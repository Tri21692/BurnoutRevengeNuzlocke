# Big Walk Teleport

Teleporting, movement toys, helper tools and harmless pranks for Big Walk. Everything except the outfit disco works when you're a guest in someone else's lobby.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **F7** | Open or close the mod window (frees the mouse while open) |
| **F5** | Show or hide your X / Y / Z |
| **D-pad down** or **,** | Teleport to wherever you're looking |
| **D-pad up** or **.** | Go back to where you were before your last teleport |
| **F1** | Flight |
| **F2** | Moon mode |
| **F3** | Sonic speed |
| **F4** | Rocket launch |
| **F6** | Friend markers |
| **F10** | Auto-walk |
| **I** | Hide your own model (only on your screen) |
| **B** | Voice of god |
| **O** | Outfit disco (host only) |
| **F9** | Write all of the game's control bindings to the log |

## The F7 window

- **Teleport:** type coordinates (leave Y blank to land on the ground), keep five saved spots, and go back to before your last teleport.
- **Fun:** hide your model, voice of god (your voice at full volume for everyone, wherever they are), and outfit disco for you or everyone (host only).
- **Troll:** kidnap a friend you're carrying to a random spot, drop & vanish, head swivel, narcolepsy, and phantom typist.
- **Move:** flight (jump goes up, crouch goes down, sprint goes fast), moon mode, sonic speed, and rocket launch.
- **Helper:** friend markers through walls, auto-walk, and a button to teleport to each friend.

## Settings

Every key and strength is in `BepInEx/config/tri21692.BigWalkTeleport.cfg`. Gale's config editor shows them under **BigWalkTeleport**.

## Good to know

- **Guests:** pranks and movement only change your own character, or a friend you're carrying the normal way. Outfit disco needs you to be the host.
- **Hiding your model** only changes your own screen.
- **Tuning:** movement numbers are first guesses; adjust them in the config if they feel off.
- **Problems:** send the BepInEx log (`BepInEx/LogOutput.log`). Every teleport and toggle is logged.
